**BentoBar 2.2.1-notch.6**: no more empty pill, and on phones the bar sits in the screen's corner with matching curves.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your device and open it (allow your browser or Files to install apps if asked; upgrades directly over `2.2.1-notch.*` and `1.7.1-notch.*`, or use **Setup › Check now** in the app).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.
3. *(Optional)* To read per-app CPU/memory lists, hardware temperatures, battery cycles/health, or accessory batteries, allow **Shizuku** under **Setup › Shizuku** (works over ADB or root) or allow root (`su`) when your root manager prompts.

### What's new
- **Fixed: the strip showing only its pill, with the items gone until you tapped it.** When the status bar hid and came back (a charger going in, the shade, full screen), the items faded in from transparent and on some phones the fade stopped at its first frame. Items now come back whole, and the strip is redrawn as soon as it shows.
- **Phones with a camera cutout:**
  - The pill sits in the screen's top-left corner, as far from the side as from the top, when nothing else is shown at the left.
  - Its corners follow the screen's own rounded corners (the screen's radius less the gap), instead of round capsule ends.
- **Includes everything from `2.2.1-notch.5` and `2.2.1-notch.4`:** the leftmost free spot, the bar never over the camera, 3 items on a phone (1 beside the Now Bar), two-digit slots, the Device link giving way to Quick Share, and the Device link tile.
