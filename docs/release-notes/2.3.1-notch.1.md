**BentoBar 2.3.1-notch.1**: merges upstream BentoBar **2.3.0** and **2.3.1** while keeping all fork features (One UI styling, pairing card & two-way device battery link, Shizuku + root sensors, phone notch corner pills, external taskbar bar, home widget, and in-app updates).

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your device and open it (allow your browser or Files to install apps if asked; upgrades directly over `2.2.1-notch.*` and `1.7.1-notch.*`, or use **Setup › Check now** in the app).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.
3. *(Optional)* To read per-app CPU/memory lists, hardware temperatures, battery cycles/health, or accessory batteries, allow **Shizuku** under **Setup › Shizuku** (works over ADB or root) or allow root (`su`) when your root manager prompts.

### What's new
- **Android 17 (API 37) SDK:** sets both `minSdk` and `targetSdk` to API 37 (Android 17).
- **Upstream BentoBar 2.3.0 & 2.3.1:**
  - **Calendar & Next meeting:** unified meeting classification across calendars, **Setup › Calendar events** card (choose calendars, solo meetings with call links, unanswered invitations, free events, and excluded title words), Next meeting options (today vs. any day, title + time vs. time alone), month-view meeting actions, debounced calendar updates, and keeping good calendar readings when a refresh query fails.
  - **Bar & SystemUI:** scans only SystemUI status bar windows, defers color sampling until a strip is actually on view, filters benign input/SIM configuration changes without resetting popups, and skips redundant layout/chip/trace work.
  - **Items & reliability:** Screen time uses API 35 `UsageEventsQuery` event filtering, Heat samples CPU load on demand while preserving chip/root thermals, Sound/Notices/Flight/Fx/Timer/Undo stability fixes, and privacy-safe logging.
- **Includes everything from `2.2.1-notch.7` and earlier:** One UI icons/fonts/styling, pairing card (`LinkCard`) and two-way cross-device battery link, Quick Share coexistence, phone cutout corner pill placement, external-display taskbar bar, glass blur controls, Shizuku + root telemetry, home screen battery widget, content providers, and in-app GitHub updates.
