**BentoBar 2.2.1-notch.4**: the bar on phones with a camera cutout, the device link giving way to Quick Share, and a Device link tile.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your device and open it (allow your browser or Files to install apps if asked; upgrades directly over `2.2.1-notch.*` and `1.7.1-notch.*`, or use **Setup › Check now** in the app).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.
3. *(Optional)* To read per-app CPU/memory lists, hardware temperatures, battery cycles/health, or accessory batteries, allow **Shizuku** under **Setup › Shizuku** (works over ADB or root) or allow root (`su`) when your root manager prompts.

### What's new
- **Phones with a camera cutout:**
  - The strip never stands over the camera any more. The cutout takes room like a status bar icon, so an item is no longer hidden behind the camera with only its pill showing.
  - The bar's two ends (rounded corners, a chip's own icon) no longer count as room.
  - At most **3 items** on a phone's bar, and **1** while the Now Bar's chip (a call, media, a timer) is up. That one item shows as you set it, even if it is wider than the room beside the chip; the others come back with ‹ when the chip goes.
  - A strip whose items all had no room is no longer drawn as an empty pill.
- **Steadier widths:** every number in an item's text keeps room for at least two digits, so the bar moves only when an item comes or goes, not when a value passes 9.
- **Device link and AirDrop:** the link to your other device drops as soon as the share sheet or Quick Share opens (and while Apple's peer-to-peer Wi-Fi is up), so Quick Share can reach Apple devices. It links again a minute after you are done. This uses the Usage access Screen time already asks for.
- **Device link tile** (new Quick Settings tile): opens the device picker, or unlinks while linked. **Device batteries › Link to … again** now links to the last device by itself, also after a restart (it remembers that device's name; scanning for it asks for *Nearby devices*), and opens the picker only if it isn't found.
