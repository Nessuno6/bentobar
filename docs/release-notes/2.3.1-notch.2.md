**BentoBar 2.3.1-notch.2**: simplifies cross-device battery linking to Google's Cross-Device picker, lets you remove saved charging cases, and prevents single-digit CPU percentages from resizing the bar.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your device and open it (allow your browser or Files to install apps if asked; upgrades directly over `2.3.1-notch.1`, `2.2.1-notch.*`, and `1.7.1-notch.*`, or use **Setup › Check now** in the app).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.
3. *(Optional)* To read per-app CPU/memory lists, hardware temperatures, battery cycles/health, or accessory batteries, allow **Shizuku** under **Setup › Shizuku** (works over ADB or root) or allow root (`su`) when your root manager prompts.

### What's new
- **Cross-device battery link:**
  - Removed the custom One UI pairing card; both the **Device batteries** menu and the **Device link** Quick Settings tile now open Google's Cross-Device picker directly.
  - Fixed **Unlink** so both devices close their channels cleanly and stay unlinked until linked again.
  - Fixed re-linking channel cleanup and updated the link status to show **Linked** only while fresh battery data is actively arriving (showing **Waiting for …** / **Waiting…** or **Paused for sharing** otherwise, with **Unlink** available in each state).
- **Remove saved charging cases:** added a `×` remove button to each saved charging case in the **Device batteries** popup and enabled **Forget** for saved cases in **Widget settings**.
- **Stable CPU width:** single-digit CPU percentages (`0%`–`9%`) now pad with a digit-width figure space so the bar and shared items do not resize when CPU load crosses 10%.
