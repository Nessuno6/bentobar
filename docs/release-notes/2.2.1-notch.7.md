**BentoBar 2.2.1-notch.7**: BentoBar's own pairing card, and a device link that shows the other side's batteries however it was made.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your device and open it (allow your browser or Files to install apps if asked; upgrades directly over `2.2.1-notch.*` and `1.7.1-notch.*`, or use **Setup › Check now** in the app).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.
3. *(Optional)* To read per-app CPU/memory lists, hardware temperatures, battery cycles/health, or accessory batteries, allow **Shizuku** under **Setup › Shizuku** (works over ADB or root) or allow root (`su`) when your root manager prompts.

### What's new
- **Pairing card:** the Device link tile and Device batteries' Link open BentoBar's own card instead of Google's device picker: a One UI card at the bottom over the screen blurred behind it (Samsung's blur on Galaxy devices). Your devices appear as they are found, with an icon for a phone, tablet or watch and "Linked" beside the one you are linked to; a tap links. **Other devices** still opens Google's picker, which is also what opens when the scan can't run (no *Nearby devices* permission).
- **Fixed: linked, but no batteries.** A link made from the card or from "Link to … again" reached only the device it was made on; the other device never accepted it, so its batteries never showed.
  - Each device now listens to the other both ways, whichever of them linked first, or both at once.
  - While Device batteries is on screen and isn't linked, it looks for the device it was last linked to (a short scan, at once and then every 2 minutes, silently) and links back. Not after you unlink, and not while Quick Share or AirDrop needs the radio.
- Fixed: "Link to … again" looked for devices with the wrong scan, and found nothing.
- **Includes everything from `2.2.1-notch.6` and earlier:** the bar in the screen's corner on phones, the empty pill fix, the Device link giving way to Quick Share, and the Device link tile.
