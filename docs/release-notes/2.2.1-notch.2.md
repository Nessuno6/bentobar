**BentoBar 2.2.1-notch.2**: fixes silent hangs and connection timeouts when linking another device.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your device and open it (allow your browser or Files to install apps if asked; upgrades directly over `2.2.1-notch.1` and `1.7.1-notch.*`).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.
3. *(Optional)* To read per-app CPU/memory lists, hardware temperatures, battery cycles/health, or accessory batteries, allow **Shizuku** under **Setup › Shizuku** (works over ADB or root) or allow root (`su`) when your root manager prompts.

### What's new
- **Fixed cross-device battery link hangs (`play-services-dtdi` `16.0.0-beta03` + `LinkActivity` / `Link` hardening):**
  - Upgraded `play-services-dtdi` to `16.0.0-beta03` so failed `createDevicePickerIntent` calls settle cleanly with a status instead of crashing inside the Binder stub.
  - Added an 8-second timeout, cancellation/error handling, recreation-safe awaiting state, `singleTop` retry recovery, and clear Toast messages (when Cross-Device Services are disabled, Google Play services do not respond in time, or the picker fails) to `LinkActivity`, preventing its transparent overlay from hanging and intercepting touches.
  - Added a 20-second connection handshake watchdog and failure Toast when opening a link (`link_could_not_connect`), fixed `WakeUpRequest` for `beta03`, and stopped calling `getBinder` on string extras in `Link.picked`.
- **Includes everything from `2.2.1-notch.1`:** optional Shizuku support with `su` fallback, upstream BentoBar 2.1.0–2.2.1 (Left/Middle/Right strips, 16 new items, real CPU load counters, Network menu addresses), and all fork features.
